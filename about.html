├─ about

Hi, I’m Melissa! I’m a Florida native who began painting at the age of seven, started fiddling around with HTML and CSS around the age of 12, and learned Photoshop and digital painting around the age of 15. I’ve always loved art, math, and science, but I was never really sure what I wanted to do as a profession.

I took a couple of programming classes during my undergrad at FIU, but I was mainly drawing, making installation art, and working toward my art history thesis. I was fortunate enough to study abroad in Italy and have my work featured in local Florida galleries and international exhibitions.

After graduating and getting a job in an unrelated field, I helped family and friends with their websites and graphic design projects on the side. I also loved learning to use CSS to make art and other “pointless” things. During the COVID lockdown, I took a web developer course along with CS50. I also participated weekly in CodePen’s challenges and managed to have the featured pen in the #CodePenChallenge: Book Text!

Nowadays, I’m trying to make more things than I consume. I also live a minimalist lifestyle. When I do consume, I stay away from almost all social media (though I frequent some local and hobby subreddits) and try to browse content on the small web or the Fediverse. I’ve also been catching up on classic sci-fi books and learning new recipes to make healthy, home-cooked meals.

I currently live in NC with my husband and daughter, where I’m enjoying the ride of helping a little human find her place in the world.
